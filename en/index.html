NoteRecord

Just say it, NoteRecord does the rest.

Save your notes, shopping lists and appointments with your voice instead of typing. Call your contacts and add new ones by voice. Turn meetings and classes into text as they happen.

Get it on Google Play Features Türkçe

🔒 Privacy first

  • No audio is recorded: NoteRecord never creates or stores an audio file.
  • Your notes, lists and settings are stored on your phone. If Android’s Google backup is on, notes may be backed up, encrypted, to your Google account.
  • No account, no sign-up; NoteRecord has no server.
  • Only you create a backup file, and you decide where it goes.
  • Translation happens on your phone; translated text is never sent to a server.

To be upfront: turning speech into text is done by your phone’s speech recognition service (usually Google), which may process the audio. The ads you watch to earn credits are shown through Google AdMob; there are no ads with a Supporter pack. See the privacy policy for details.

What it does

Tap the microphone, speak, and stop when you're done. NoteRecord understands what you want and puts it in the right place.

🆓

Free

NoteRecord is free. You start with 20 uses; when they run out, watch an ad whenever you like to get 5 more, with no limit on how many ads you can watch. No ads pop up on their own. A Supporter pack gives unlimited use without ads.

🎙️

By voice from the home screen

Tap the microphone and speak: a note, a calendar appointment (with a reminder), a shopping list, or a call to a saved contact. Keeps listening until you stop; no sentence is lost, even if you pause.

🛒

Smart shopping list

Say "Shopping list milk, 2 kg tomatoes" and your checklist is ready.

📅

Calendar and reminders

Say "Dentist appointment in three days"; it goes into the calendar with a reminder as early as you choose (5–60 min before).

📞

Calls and contacts by voice

Say "Call Ahmet" or "Call Mom"; the contact is found in your phone's contacts, shown with photo and name, and called. Say "Add contact Ayşe 555…" for a new contact or "Add number 555… to Ayşe" for a second number; the Contacts app opens with it filled in.

🆘

Emergency

Also for people who live alone: say "Help!"; your contact is called automatically first, then the emergency dialer opens and your location is ready to send by SMS with one tap. Off by default. It does not replace emergency services; we also suggest turning on your phone's Emergency SOS.

📝

Live note-taking (8 languages)

Writes down meetings and classes sentence by sentence, without translating, adds punctuation automatically and saves them as one note.

✨

Clean up, summarize, PDF

Clean up and summarize live notes on your phone; turn them into a PDF and share it.

💬

Two-way conversation (8 languages)

Put the phone between you; everyone speaks their own language and translations are read aloud. Translations are made by Google ML Kit’s on-device engine; they give the approximate meaning and are not perfect. Check important texts.

📤

Share on WhatsApp

Share lists with your family; add a list you receive to yours with one tap.

✈️

Offline translation

Download language packs in advance and translate without internet while travelling.

🌍

Turkish and English interface

Menus, spoken replies and voice commands work in Turkish and English; two-way conversation translation covers 8 languages.

💾

Backup

Export your notes to a file with one tap and restore them on a new phone.

🔒

Your data stays with you

No account needed. Notes are stored on your phone (if Android’s Google backup is on, they may be backed up, encrypted, to your Google account), and no audio is kept.

Our mission

NoteRecord’s goal is to make saving whatever comes to mind as easy as saying it. We record your notes, shopping lists and appointments by voice, without typing, and put them in the right place; you can also call your contacts and add new ones by voice. While doing so, we keep your data only on your phone and respect your privacy.
